ETL Flow (textual / mermaid-style)

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  A[Raw CSVs in data/] --> B[Extract]
  B --> C[Clean: cast types, remove nulls, fix timestamps]
  C --> D[Deduplicate & Enrich: TotalPrice, OrderMonth, Channel]
  D --> E[Write Parquet to etl/output/]
  E --> F[Load into Data Warehouse (SQL), create star schema]
  F --> G[Analysis & Dashboards]
```

Metrics logged: initial_row_count, after_cleaning, after_dedup, null_customer_count, records_with_negative_quantity
