GlobalShop Enterprise ETL & Star Schema Architecture

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    subgraph Raw Data Ingestion
        A1[online_retail_ii.csv] --> B1[Extract & Schema Enforcement]
        A2[olist_orders.csv] --> B2[Extract & Regional Mapping]
        A3[retailrocket_events.csv] --> B3[Extract & Event Funnel Extraction]
    end

    subgraph Data Harmonization & Quality
        B1 --> C1[Clean: Cast numeric types, filter returns/Quantity<=0, drop UnitPrice<=0]
        C1 --> C2[Standardize: Normalize timestamps to UTC InvoiceTimestamp]
        C2 --> C3[Deduplicate: Drop redundant invoice-item events]
        C3 --> C4[Impute: Map unassigned CustomerID to GUEST]
    end

    subgraph Kimball Star Schema Modeling
        C4 --> D1[DimProduct: Unique StockCode, Description, UnitPrice, surrogate ProductKey]
        C4 --> D2[DimRegion: Country mappings, surrogate RegionKey]
        C4 & D2 --> D3[DimCustomer: Unique CustomerID, Country, surrogate CustomerKey, RegionKey FK]
        C4 --> D4[DimDate: Distinct dates, DateKey YYYYMMDD, Year, Month, Day, DayOfWeek, Quarter]
        C4 & D1 & D3 & D4 & D2 --> D5[FactSales: Distributed SHA256 SalesKey, ProductKey, CustomerKey, DateKey, RegionKey, measures]
        B3 & D1 --> D6[FactFunnel: Distributed FunnelKey, VisitorID, StockCode, ProductKey, Views, AddToCart, Transactions, Converted]
    end

    subgraph Storage & Analytics
        D5 --> E1[Partitioned Parquet by Year, Month: etl/output/fact_sales]
        D6 --> E2[Parquet Funnel Table: etl/output/fact_funnel.parquet]
        D1 & D2 & D3 & D4 --> E3[Parquet Dimension Tables: etl/output/dim_*.parquet]
        E1 & E2 & E3 --> F[DuckDB In-Memory OLAP Queries & Executive BI Dashboards]
    end
```
